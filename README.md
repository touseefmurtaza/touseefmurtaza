### What I am building toward

- AI-first applications: agentic workflows, automation pipelines, retrieval, copilots, and LLM-powered product features.
- Frontend systems: Next.js, React, TypeScript, performance-minded UI, dashboards, and product-grade interfaces.
- Backend platforms: Node.js, APIs, background jobs, integrations, authentication, and production data flows.
- Cloud delivery: AWS, Docker, CI/CD, serverless patterns, observability, and deployment workflows.
- Product engineering: shipping usable software, not just demos.

### Current focus

- Building stronger AI engineering projects around agents, workflow orchestration, and support automation.
- Turning full-stack experience into reliable AI product architecture.
- Publishing practical notes and reusable examples for teams adopting AI in real software.
